02 — How I work

Governance as an accelerant

Well‑designed guardrails make it possible for regulated organisations to move faster. The friction comes when governance is invented case‑by‑case; building a clear, reusable framework avoids that.

Capability at scale

Practical experience develops judgement. Large, hands‑on exercises teach more broadly than small, closed pilots — people learn what to ask and when not to rely on AI.

Let evidence lead

Cheap, quick, safe experiments resolve questions that opinion cannot. The goal is not a single high‑profile success but an operational pattern that makes running follow‑on experiments straightforward and repeatable.
