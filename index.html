02 — How I work

Governance is an accelerant

Guardrails designed properly are the reason a regulated firm can move faster than one that has to stop and think from first principles every time. The brake feeling comes from governance invented per-project. Build it once, well, and it stops being the thing everyone routes around.

Capability spreads or it stops

A 1,200-person hackathon teaches an organisation more than a twelve-person pilot ever will. Most of what AI changes shows up as judgement — knowing what to ask it, and when not to — and judgement only develops in people who have touched the thing.

Let the evidence decide

Cheap, fast, safe experiments settle arguments that seniority and opinion can't. The real work isn't running one impressive experiment. It's making experiments boring enough that anyone can run the next fifteen.
